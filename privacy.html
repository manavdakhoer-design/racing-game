Privacy Policy

Weight-Shift Odyssey (website and Android app) · Last updated: 21 November 2026

In short: the game has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your scores, settings and progress stay on your own device. The developer does not receive any of it.

1. Who this is from

Weight-Shift Odyssey is made by an independent developer (GitHub: manavdakhoer-design). The game is available as a website at manavdakhoer-design.github.io/racing-game and as an Android app. This policy covers both.

2. What the game stores on your device

The game saves the following only on your own device (in your browser storage, or in the app's own storage):

  • your settings (sound, difficulty, accessibility and graphics options, text size, colour-vision options);
  • your best scores, times, ranks and run records;
  • the orbs you collected and the skins and trails you unlocked or equipped;
  • tutorial and hint progress;
  • saved replay data used to draw your "ghost" car (your button inputs, not video);
  • a random identifier that may be created on your device. In this version it is never sent anywhere.

None of this is personal information that identifies you, and none of it is sent to the developer. The game never asks for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera.

3. What the game does not do

  • No user accounts, sign-in or sign-up.
  • No advertising and no advertising IDs.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or tracking tools.
  • No in-app purchases. Orbs are earned by playing and have no real-money value.
  • No chat or messages between players.
  • No selling or sharing of data, because the developer does not collect any.

4. Network connections

  • Google Fonts. The game loads its heading font (Orbitron) from Google Fonts. Like any web request, this sends your IP address and basic browser or WebView information to Google so the font files can be delivered. See Google Fonts privacy FAQ. The developer does not receive this information.
  • Website hosting. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) under its own privacy statement. The developer does not see these logs.
  • Offline use. The website uses a service worker to keep a copy of the game files in your browser so it can run offline. These are the game's own files, not your personal data.

5. Google Play Games

This version of the Android app does not use Google Play Games and does not sign you in to any account. If Play Games sign-in, achievements or leaderboards are added in a future version, this policy will be updated first to describe what Google handles under Google's Privacy Policy.

6. Things you choose to share

  • Share score. When you tap a share button, the game prepares an image or text with your result and opens your device's share sheet. It goes only where you choose to send it.
  • Backup and restore. You can export your progress to a file and import it later. The file contains game progress and settings only, and is saved or shared only where you choose.

7. Daily Challenge

The Daily Challenge track is created on your device from the current date (UTC). It does not use a server. If an online daily leaderboard is added in a future version, this policy will be updated first to describe exactly what it stores, and the game will say so before anything is sent.

8. Children

The game is suitable for players aged 6 and over, including children. It collects no personal information from anyone: no name, email, location, contacts, photos or advertising ID, and it shows no ads and has no chat or accounts. Children's scores, settings and progress stay on their device. Parents can remove everything by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app (see section 9).

The only outside request is the font download described in section 4. If you have questions about how the game treats children's privacy, contact us using section 11.

9. Your choices and deleting data

  • Website: clear the site data for this website in your browser settings.
  • Android app: clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app.

Because the developer holds none of your game data, there is nothing on the developer's side to delete.

10. Changes to this policy

If the game's data practices change, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change.

11. Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue at github.com/manavdakhoer-design/racing-game/issues.
