What is stored

The game has no accounts, passwords or email. If you turn on the online board, the server keeps only:

  • a random anonymous id,
  • a racer name that the game picks (for example Racer-4F2A, or a name you pick from a fixed word list),
  • the Daily scores you submit, with the replay that proves them (kept up to 90 days),
  • a short-lived anti-abuse log that holds a daily-salted hash of your network address, never the address itself (kept 2 days).

Nothing else leaves your device. Your records, orbs and settings stay on your device.

Delete it

In the app or the game: open Settings → Online board → Delete my online data and confirm. This is the easiest way, and it works the same in the Android app and in the browser game.

From this browser: if you used the online board in the browser version of the game on this device, you can delete that data here.

Deleting removes your racer name and every score you sent. It cannot be undone. It does not touch the progress saved on your device.

If you can no longer open the game

Because there is no account, nobody, including us, can tell which anonymous id belongs to you. If you cannot reach the device, your data is removed automatically: scores and replays after 90 days, and anonymous ids that never sent a score after 30 days.
